Flight Board Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Flight Board is a live golf scorecard app. You and the people you play with keep score on your own phones and see each other’s rounds as they happen. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what is never done with it.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number. Used to sign you in and to match you to rounds you’ve been invited to. Nothing else.
  • Your name and golf handicap, as entered by you or by the person organising your round.
  • Your scores for the rounds you play, and any side games or wagers recorded within them.
  • Courses you star as favourites, so they load without a lookup.

Contacts

If you choose to add playing partners from your phone’s contacts, Flight Board reads your contact list on your device, at the moment you tap. Your contact list is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted. Only the name and number of a person you specifically select is saved, and only so they can be added to your round and sent an invite.

Mobile information is never shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or trade mobile phone numbers. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, and information sharing with subcontractors who support the service (see below) specifically excludes any sharing for marketing purposes.

Text messages

  • What we send: a six-digit verification code when you sign in.
  • Message frequency: varies, and is driven entirely by you — typically one message each time you sign in on a new device. Most people receive a handful of messages a year. We do not send marketing or promotional texts of any kind.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help, or email the address below.

Invitations to a round are sent from your own phone, through your own messaging app, when you choose to send them. Flight Board does not send them on your behalf and does not text people who haven’t been invited by someone they know.

Who else sees your data

  • People in your round. Golf is played in a group, so the players in a round see each other’s names, handicaps and scores. That is the point of the app. People outside your round do not.
  • Our service providers, and only to run the service: Supabase (database and sign-in) and Twilio (delivering verification codes). They process this data on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell your data. There is no advertising in Flight Board and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Money

Flight Board records who owes whom for side games and settles it to the fewest payments. It never moves money. No payment details are collected, stored or processed.

Keeping and deleting your data

Rounds are kept so last week’s card still exists when you want to look at it. You can delete a round from within the app, which deletes its scores, sign-offs and side-game records. To delete your account and everything attached to it, email the address below and we will do it within 30 days.

Children

Flight Board is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes
