Flight Board

A live scoring leaderboard without the math.

Everyone’s round as it happens, the games being played inside it, and you keep your own score. No more paper scorecards, and no more working out who owes whom in the car park.

What it does

  • Live scoring across the group — everyone enters their own, everyone sees the board
  • Real course cards: par, yardage and stroke index for every hole
  • Teams drawn balanced off handicaps, gross or net
  • Side games — Wolf, closest to the pin, longest drive, team challenge
  • One settle-up at the end, netted to the fewest payments

Signing in

Flight Board signs you in with your mobile number and a one-time code — no password. Message frequency varies, normally one text per sign-in. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.
