1FLIGHT BOARD — live multiplayer scorecard · Gladstan Golf Club, Payson UT
ASSUMPTIONS

One-handed, gloved, in full sun: every primary target is ≥64px, the hole number and the score are the two largest things on screen, and no screen in the round loop requires the keyboard. Typing happens only twice — your phone number at sign-in, and a course search.

SYSTEM

Modernist, unmodified: zero radius, 2px rules doing all the dividing, Archivo throughout, ink on #f3f2f2 with red reserved for the one live action and the one number that matters. The scorecard grid is the modular grid — the design system and the artifact happen to be the same object.

STATE MODEL

Scores are optimistic-local first, queued when offline, and the leaderboard is the same object rendered at three zooms: group, field, side game. Sign-off freezes the card and stamps both the player and the marker.
